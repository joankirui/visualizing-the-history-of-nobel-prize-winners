# Visualizing the History of Nobel Prize Winners

## Project Overview

The Nobel Prize has been one of the most prestigious international awards since 1901. Each year, awards are presented in chemistry, literature, physics, physiology or medicine, economics, and peace.

This project explores Nobel Prize winner data from 1901 to 2023 to investigate historical patterns and trends among laureates. The analysis focuses on Nobel Prize categories, winner demographics, nationality, gender, and repeat recipients.

## Objectives

- Explore the history of Nobel Prize winners from 1901 to 2023
- Analyze the proportion of US-born Nobel Prize winners across decades
- Identify the decade and category with the highest proportion of female laureates
- Identify the first woman to receive a Nobel Prize
- Identify individuals and organizations that have received multiple Nobel Prizes
- Explore patterns and trends within the Nobel Prize data

## Key Questions

The analysis investigates the following questions:

1. Which decade had the highest ratio of US-born Nobel Prize winners to total winners?
2. Which decade and Nobel Prize category had the highest proportion of female laureates?
3. Who was the first woman to receive a Nobel Prize, and in which category?
4. Which individuals or organizations have won more than one Nobel Prize?

## Dataset

The dataset contains information about Nobel Prize winners from 1901 to 2023.

The data was obtained from the Nobel Prize API and is provided in the `nobel.csv` file in the `data` folder.

Key information includes:

- Nobel Prize year
- Prize category
- Laureate name
- Birth country
- Gender
- Other information about Nobel Prize recipients
